A new studio, a careful eye

The Rohit Photography began in 2025 in Firojpur Gandawali, Gajraula. I started it because every family in our area deserves wedding pictures they are proud to show for decades.

I work closely with each family, so nothing important is missed: the bride's first look, the elders' blessings, the groom's turban being tied. Our edits keep your faces, your outfits and your day exactly as they were.

  • Owner-led: you deal with Rohit directly
  • Photos and cinematic film from one team
  • Careful colour and black-and-white edits
  • Enquiry-based quotes, no hidden charges
  • Local to Gajraula and nearby towns
